How the calculation works

EBITDA = Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses
EBITDA Margin = EBITDA ÷ Revenue × 100
Suggested Multiple = Industry Base Multiple + Business Quality Adjustments
Enterprise Value = EBITDA × Suggested Multiple
Equity Value = Enterprise Value − Debt + Cash
Multiple ranges shown are illustrative estimates. Actual valuation should consider comparable transactions, market conditions, financial quality and due diligence.
