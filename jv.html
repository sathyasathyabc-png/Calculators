01 · Profile

Research driven by mechanism.

Combining structural, biochemical and functional approaches to understand how proteins protect genome integrity and influence host–virus interactions.

Ananda Ayyappan Jaguva Vasudevan is a molecular biologist with research experience spanning DNA repair, transcription-associated processes, antiviral restriction factors, viral epitranscriptomics and cancer epigenetics.

His work integrates protein structure determination with biochemical and cellular assays to investigate molecular mechanisms. At NIEHS/NIH, his current research focuses on the DNA repair and transcription factor ZATT/ZNF451, including its roles in TOP2 DNA–protein crosslink repair and RNA/DNA hybrid cleavage.

He earned a Ph.D. in Biology (Dr. rer. nat.) from Heinrich Heine University, Düsseldorf, Germany, with the distinction Summa cum laude.

02 · Research areas

Questions at the molecular level.

Selected areas represented in the research experience and publications documented in the CV.

⌬

ZATT / ZNF451 biology

Structural and functional investigation of a DNA repair and transcription factor, including TOP2 DNA–protein crosslink repair and RNA/DNA hybrid cleavage.

⌁

APOBEC3 restriction factors

Mechanistic studies of APOBEC3 family members, substrate specificity, antiviral restriction and viral counteraction mechanisms.

◈

Protein structure & biochemistry

Integrative approaches including crystallography, electrophoretic mobility shift assays (EMSA) and enzymatic assays.

◎

Host–virus interactions

Research on HIV and other viruses, viral epitranscriptomic modification, replication and host restriction factors.

▦

Cancer epigenetics

Studies of histone deacetylases in urothelial carcinoma, gene expression profiles and cellular responses to HDAC inhibition.

⌘

Integrated molecular analysis

Application of transcriptomic, proteomic and functional assays to investigate molecular regulation and cellular phenotypes.
