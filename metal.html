How the calculation works

1. Convert weight to grams. For example, 1 kg = 1,000 g.

2. Calculate volume: volume (cm³) = mass (g) ÷ density (g/cm³).

3. Calculate metal cross-sectional area. Solid wire: π × diameter² ÷ 4. Hollow pipe: π × (outside diameter² − inside diameter²) ÷ 4.

4. Calculate length: length (m) = volume (cm³) × 1,000 ÷ area (mm²) ÷ 1,000.

Length = usable volume ÷ cross-sectional area

Densities are typical approximations and alloys vary. Estimates exclude process effects, dimensional tolerances and any losses beyond the percentage entered.
