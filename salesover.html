i What are Operating Expenses? Operating expenses are the normal day-to-day costs required to run the business.

Examples:
• Salaries and wages
• Rent
• Electricity and utilities
• Advertising and marketing
• Office expenses
• Insurance
• Routine repairs and maintenance
Not included:
Capital expenditure such as purchasing machinery, buildings, vehicles or other long-term assets.

Capital expenditure is normally recorded as an asset and is not treated as an operating expense. Depreciation/amortization may be recognized as an expense over the asset's useful life.
