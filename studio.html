HOW TO COMPOSE · QUICK MANUAL

1 · Start with a groove

Click Load demo groove or choose a preset below. Press Play to hear the arrangement. Adjust tempo between 40 and 220 BPM to change the speed.

2 · Build a beat

Each track row is an instrument; its 16 numbered columns are timing steps. Click any square to turn a note on or off. Try drum steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 for a basic pulse, then add extra hits between them.

3 · Write a melody

Select an instrument in Edit track. In the piano roll, rows are pitches and columns are timing steps. Click a cell to add a note and click it again to remove it. Higher rows sound higher. Use Add scale notes for a quick starting melody.

4 · Layer instruments

Use drums for rhythm, bass for low notes, piano or guitar for harmony, and flute, violin, saxophone, trumpet or synth for melodies. Use M to mute a track, S to solo it, and the track volume slider to balance the mix.

5 · Record live playing

Choose a track, press Record live, then play the on-screen keys or keyboard keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K. Notes are added to the selected track. Click the recording button again to stop recording.

6 · Save or export

Save project downloads a JSON project file. Use Load project to reopen it. Export WAV makes uncompressed audio; Export MP3 makes a smaller audio file and may need internet to load its encoder.

A simple first song

Set tempo to 100–120 BPM → load Pop groove → press Play → select Piano → add notes on steps 1, 5, 9, and 13 → select Bass and add low notes → mute/solo tracks to balance them → save the project → export WAV or MP3.

Sound is synthesized by the browser, not from realistic studio samples. Live recording captures notes played inside this app; it does not record microphone or external instrument audio. Save your project often.
