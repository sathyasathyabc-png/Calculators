Welcome to your Subscription Manager

1. Add subscriptions
Record service, price, billing cycle, currency, renewal date and payment method.
2. Monitor recurring spend
The summary converts weekly, monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and yearly charges into monthly and annual estimates. One-time items are excluded.
3. Find renewals
Upcoming renewals appear in the right panel. Search and filter the main list to find an item quickly.
4. Back up your data
Export creates a JSON backup. Import restores a backup and replaces the current list after confirmation.

Accessibility: keyboard-operable controls, labels, clear status text and responsive layout. This tool does not connect to providers or send automatic reminders.
